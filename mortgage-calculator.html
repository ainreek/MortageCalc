Down Payment Impact

Putting 20% or more down eliminates PMI and instantly lowers your monthly payment. Even an extra 5% can save thousands over 30 years.

📅

15 vs. 30 Year Term

A 15-year mortgage cuts your total interest roughly in half, but increases monthly payments by ~40%. Great if cash flow allows it.

💡

Rate Shopping Pays Off

A 0.5% lower interest rate on a $350k loan saves over $35,000 in interest across 30 years. Always compare at least 3 lenders.
